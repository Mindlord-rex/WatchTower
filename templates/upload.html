How WatchTower Video Processing Works

  1. Video Storage: The file is verified and saved to the local workspace repository.
  2. Frame Extraction: OpenCV extracts frames at the chosen sampling interval without loading full video into RAM.
  3. YOLOv8 Detection: The fine-tuned PRW model detects person instances in each frame.
  4. CLIP Vectorization: Detected crops are preprocessed and encoded into 512-D vectors with CLIP ViT-B/32.
  5. Persistence: Embeddings are stored in persistent ChromaDB; metadata and timestamps are indexed in SQLite.
